# Evidence-to-conclusion methods

These are competing-method selection rules, not results for any fixed benchmark case. Always prefer concrete source definitions, and disclose limitations rather than filling gaps with plausible values.

## 1. Evidence and comparison contract

Begin with experimental unit, repeated-reading hierarchy, sample/batch/channel/instrument identities, timestamps, x/y units, controls, method validity range and censorship. **Repeat readings of one unit do not create independent n**. A condition totally confounded with a batch/device cannot be statistically separated from that batch/device. References: [Vaux et al., 2012](https://doi.org/10.1038/embor.2012.36), [Hurlbert, 1984](https://doi.org/10.2307/1942661), [NIST blocking](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pri/section3/pri332.htm).

Raw record hashes, source parser/version and exact locators are evidence infrastructure. XLSX cached values are not recalculated formulas; PDF pages, workbook formula/cache views and independently computed results are three different statuses. References: [openpyxl data_only](https://openpyxl.readthedocs.io/en/stable/tutorial.html), [SciPy loadmat](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.io.loadmat.html), [MathWorks MAT versions](https://www.mathworks.com/help/matlab/import_export/mat-file-versions.html).

## 2. Descriptive contrast and robust anomalies

Compute each unit's effect before pooling. Paired differences within the same batch/split/sample are preferred when identity is established; keep both original-unit difference and a clearly defined relative measure. Do not turn near-zero denominators into dramatic ratios. Retain mean, median, range, dispersion type and both `n_units` and `n_readings`.

The screening residual is `|x_i - median(reference_without_unit_i)| / (1.4826*MAD(reference_without_unit_i))`. The 1.4826 factor is a normal-consistency convention, **not** proof of normality; 3.5 is a declared exploratory screen, not a universal source cutoff or p-value. Use whole-unit leave-out to avoid masking by the tested unit. Check clusters and alternative groupings; a LOO mask does not eliminate other outliers. On zero MAD report raw discrepancy/tie structure and use a source-specified resolution if available; never append epsilon to create gigantic standardized scores.

Multivariate/curve residual search should stay within compatible groups. Context/batch/channel residuals can be conditioned through a declared median-polish or robust group contrast, but a correlated configuration is not proof of which condition caused it. No automatic deleting/winsorizing. References: [Rousseeuw & Hubert, 2011](https://wis.kuleuven.be/stat/robust/papers/2011/rousseeuwhubert-robuststatisticsforoutlierdetectio.pdf), [NIST anomaly guidance](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda35h.htm).

Only a genuinely independent calibration sample under an exchangeable design can supply conformal outlier p-values; do not relabel LOO ranks or robust-z values as p. Constraint: minimum attainable conformal p is `1/(n_calibration+1)`. See [Bates et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1214/22-AOS2244). [ASA p-value statement](https://www.amstat.org/asa/files/pdfs/p-valuestatement.pdf) forbids treating `p>0.05` as no effect.

## 3. Raw curve analysis

For each source-backed x/y sequence preserve raw points, sampling coverage, sign and phase. Direct features include peak and its first/all tied indices, endpoint contrast, raw-order trapezoid area and source-specified interval slope. A transition from a fitted segmented line is `candidate_transition`, not automatically yield; a high regression R² does not prove elasticity. No observed post-peak section is different from no physical post-peak behavior.

For at least five compatible traces, use common-overlap 201-point linearly interpolated curves only for functional screening. Modified band depth at each included grid point is `#{1 <= j < k <= n: min(fj,fk) <= fi <= max(fj,fk)} / C(n,2)`, averaged over the grid. Thus the pair count includes ties and pairs involving `i`; it excludes pairs of two other curves when neither contains `fi` and is not an arbitrary leave-one-out quantile. The central 50% band and 1.5-times-band expansion produce exploratory candidates. Calculate deviation in raw y units against the leave-one-curve-out pointwise median; retain magnitude and shape separately. No blanket confidence level or fault cause follows from 1.5. Do not enumerate enormous dynamic-time-warp or unconstrained model searches in this fixed budget. References: [Lopez-Pintado & Romo, 2009](https://doi.org/10.1198/jasa.2009.0108), [Sun & Genton, 2011](https://doi.org/10.1198/jcgs.2011.09224), [FAST-MBD](https://doi.org/10.1002/sta4.8), [segmented model](https://doi.org/10.1002/sim.1545).

## 4. Requested computational estimator experiment

Only reexecute what the original case actually specifies; do not manufacture canonical Iris accuracy. Save actual dataset/array hashes, complete `get_params`, train/test IDs, shared splits, split and estimator random states, all predictions, warnings, versions and fitted-run states. Fit preprocessing within each training fold. If new settings produce another result, it is a sensitivity/new-experiment result, not a replacement for the reported one. References: [Iris versions](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.datasets.load_iris.html), [leakage](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html), [nested CV](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/auto_examples/model_selection/plot_nested_cross_validation_iris.html).

For each split `j`, `accuracy_j = correct_j / n_test_j` and paired `delta_j = accuracy_Aj - accuracy_Bj`. Report all folds, the signed mean paired change `mean(delta)*100` in percentage points, median/range of paired deltas, wins/ties/losses, and integer correct counts when recoverable. If `mean(abs(delta))*100` is additionally useful, label it mean absolute paired change explicitly rather than conflating it with signed `mean(delta)`. Distinguish percentage-point change, relative accuracy change and relative error reduction. Average by fold sizes for pooled scores when sizes differ. Fold ID is not a temporal or dose axis.

Five-fold scores share training samples; no ordinary paired t `SD/sqrt(5)` CI or fold bootstrap heals that dependence. Five repeated random splits are also not Dietterich's `5x2cv`. If hyperparameters/features were selected on the tested folds, say so and propose nested evaluation plus truly untouched external data rather than reporting those scores as unbiased generalization. References: [Dietterich, 1998](https://direct.mit.edu/neco/article/10/7/1895/6224/Approximate-Statistical-Tests-for-Comparing), [Nadeau & Bengio, 2003](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1024068626366), [Bengio & Grandvalet, 2004](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v5/grandvalet04a.html), [Cawley & Talbot, 2010](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v11/cawley10a.html).

## 5. Paper-guided analytical chemistry

Identify analyte/salt basis, matrix, candidate/reference method and intended use from actual paper/workbook. Regression and concentration calculations retain axis, weighting, blank/background, dilution, mass basis and paper-defined formulas:

- nonlinear-free OLS with intercept: `R² = 1 - SSE/SST`; do not claim universal accuracy from high r/R²;
- known-known recovery: `100*C_found/C_nominal`; matched spike recovery may require `(C_spiked-C_unspiked)/C_added` only on a common mass/volume basis;
- sample RSD uses `s/mean` with actual repeat hierarchy and unit; do not relabel injections, scans or independent preparations interchangeably;
- `LOD=3.3*sigma/S`, `LOQ=10*sigma/S` only when the source method defines compatible sigma and S and supplied inputs permit recomputation. Otherwise label reported-only or not estimable. LOD is not LOQ, and LOD/2 is not a legitimate fill-in;
- paired-method correlation is not agreement; differences, their source and intended-use acceptance criteria must be examined. Absence of a significant difference is not equivalence.

No universal validation acceptance range applies to every drug/method/matrix. A method comparison is not a product release test, bioequivalence study or chemical structure proof. References: [ICH Q2(R2), corrected 2025](https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/ICH_Q2%28R2%29_Guideline_2023_1130_ErrorCorrection_2025.pdf), [ICH Q14](https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/ICH_Q14_Guideline_2023_1130_ErrorCorrection_2025.pdf), [Bland–Altman review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4470095/), [FDA analytical procedures](https://www.fda.gov/media/87801/download).

## 6. Cause competition and actionable next steps

Rank causes using local controlled evidence, direct log/calculation chains, independent consistent observations, and finally mechanistic speculation. For each candidate list necessary predictions, contrary observations, equivalent rivals, confounders, and one action that gives different outcomes for opposing candidates. A co-located batch/channel or field-plausible narrative remains an association without an actual discriminating intervention. References: [Strong inference](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.146.3642.347), [Hernan & Robins](https://www.hsph.harvard.edu/miguel-hernan/wp-content/uploads/sites/1268/2024/04/hernanrobins_WhatIf_26apr24.pdf).

Check the strongest reverse claim using a small, source-justified sensitivity set (leave-one-batch/unit, unnormalized versus normalized, explicit alternative identity), not a post-hoc search for a preferred result. Report instability. Every follow-up names input, actor, parameters/ranges, observable pass/fail criterion, hierarchy of independent repeats, control, cost/time basis and approval gates. Do not automatically assign `n=3` or a numerical information gain without variance/generative-model evidence. Methods borrowed from [negative-control considerations](https://dash.harvard.edu/bitstreams/7312037d-b555-6bd4-e053-0100007fdf3b/download) require both lack of causal pathway and similar bias structure.

The implementation's novelty is a **change-aware evidence chain**: source/object → numerical computation → claim → competing explanation → decisive check, with retained conflicts and explicit invalidation after a changed input or conclusion. This is an engineering integration of established methods, not a claim of a new statistical theorem or a measured improvement over the base Agent. Platform comparisons supply any effectiveness evidence.

## 7. Native MATLAB and explicit mapping limitations

Legacy MATLAB cell/struct containers are expanded only through bounded nested two-dimensional numeric leaves. Retain the original path, shape, dtype, one-based positions and container chain. Current bounds are four nesting levels, 10,000 container elements, and five million expanded numeric values per parent container; nonnumeric values and unsupported arrays remain summaries with explicit limitations. HDF5 MATLAB support needs an actually available `h5py`: direct one-level object references to two-dimensional numeric datasets are implemented, but nested references and layout-specific HDF5 structs are not automatically resolved.

A `.m` file is never executed. Only corresponding constant declarations that explicitly supply sample ID, source file, MAT variable and column are emitted through `source_curve_mappings`; group must also be explicit before it becomes a comparison group. Incomplete or unrecognized scripts remain unresolved, not a license to pair by file order, attractive curve shapes or the expectation of 197 samples. A metadata workbook or an equally explicit declared relation can supply the identity and response roles instead. A two-column array must not automatically yield two traces: establish displacement/shared-axis versus response, retain the stored orientation, and explicitly record why an axis was selected or why it remains unresolved.

If raw data live in another representation, say exactly which variables/indices were read and which remain unavailable. Headerless scalar arrays and three-or-higher-dimensional arrays are not silently flattened into physical bending curves. Original MAT/MATLAB mapping identifies computational observations; it does not add wet-lab replicates or prove a strength, fracture or elasticity interpretation.
